HOUSE STYLE (standing rules; applies to every deck, on top of the rules above):
- The deck is projected in a room: few words per slide, large text, the point carried by structure and charts, not paragraphs.
- Visual storytelling: the action titles read top to bottom must tell the whole argument; every slide must flow from the previous one.
- No empty-looking slides and no stuffed slides: pick the archetype whose capacity matches the content (3 facts -> keyTrends with 3 items, not a 6-row table).
- Colours come from the analysed company's brand (meta.brandPrimary), never a generic palette.
- Avoid typical AI writing tics: no "leverage", "unlock", "robust", "seamless", "delve", "in today's fast-paced", no rhetorical questions as titles, no exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Numbers beat adjectives: "26% CEE share" not "strong position".
- Every claim traceable: if a number is derived, the appendix shows the calculation.
